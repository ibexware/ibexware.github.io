Facetory — Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

Your photos never leave your device. Facetory finds and groups faces, and renders your video, entirely on your iPhone. No photo, no face, and no video you create is ever uploaded — not to us, not to anyone.

Facetory does show ads, and those ads are personalized. That involves tracking, and the app asks your permission for it. Both of these things are true at the same time: we track for advertising, and we never upload your photos.

What we collect

Nothing. Facetory has no account system, no analytics, and no server of our own. There is nowhere for us to store information about you, because we do not operate a backend at all.

Your photos

Facetory asks for access to your photo library so it can look for faces. Everything it does with those photos happens on your device, using Apple's on-device frameworks:

  • Face detection and grouping run locally on your iPhone's Neural Engine.
  • Working copies of scanned photos are written to Facetory's own private storage on the device, and are cleared when the app next launches.
  • Your finished video is written to your device, and goes to your photo library only when you tap Save, or to another app only when you choose to share it.

You can change or revoke photo access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Advertising

Facetory shows banner ads through Google AdMob. Those ads are personalized, which means Google may use an advertising identifier and information about your device and app usage to choose them. Google's own description of what its Mobile Ads SDK collects is here: AdMob data disclosure.

The first time an ad is shown, Facetory asks for permission to track using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and where required it also shows Google's consent form. You can decline. If you do, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's advertising SDK never receives your photos, your faces, or the videos you make. It has no access to them.

Children

Facetory is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, please get in touch.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a subscription is added — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
