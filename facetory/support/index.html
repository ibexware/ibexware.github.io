Facetory — Support

Facetory turns your photos of the same person over time into a face-aligned video you can share.

Need help? Email ibexware@outlook.com. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version usually saves a round trip.

Why does it need my photos?

To find faces in them. All of that happens on your iPhone — your photos are never uploaded. See the privacy policy for the detail.

The scan didn't find anyone

Facetory scans your most recent photos and needs faces it can recognise as the same person more than once. Two things commonly get in the way: photos stored only in iCloud and not on the device are skipped, and photos where the face is small, turned away, or in deep shadow may not be detected.

It split one person into several

That's expected — the grouping works by similarity, not identity, so one person can appear as two or three. On the People screen, use the menu in the top right, choose Combine People, select the groups that are the same person, and combine them.

Can I remove the watermark?

No. Every video Facetory makes carries a small "Made with Facetory" mark in the corner, and there is no way to remove it.

Why are there ads?

They pay for the app. Facetory shows a banner while you work; the ads are personalized, and you can decline tracking when asked — you'll still see ads, just less relevant ones.

Which devices are supported?

Facetory needs iOS 27 or later, because the on-device face grouping it relies on is only available there. On an older system the app will say so rather than misbehave.
